# POUR for Branding Systems and CX

The emotional accessibility principles of accessible branding systems and customer experiences.

POUR is a practical framework for designing experiences that are more inclusive, understandable, and emotionally resonant. In this repository, the focus is on applying the POUR accessibility principles—Perceivable, Operable, Understandable, and Robust—to branding systems and customer experience strategy.

## Why this repository exists

Accessible brands are not just legally compliant or visually consistent. They are emotionally intelligent, cognitively clear, and usable across contexts. This project explores how brand systems, messaging, journeys, and customer touchpoints can be designed with accessibility in mind from the start.

The aim is to help teams create experiences that:

- feel welcoming and emotionally safe
- reduce friction for diverse audiences
- communicate clearly across channels and contexts
- remain resilient as products, platforms, and audiences evolve

## The POUR lens

### Perceivable
Design experiences that people can recognize, access, and interpret regardless of their sensory or contextual conditions.

- clear visual hierarchy and contrast
- accessible content and media
- inclusive representation in tone, imagery, and language

### Operable
Make experiences usable without unnecessary effort or unnecessary barriers.

- intuitive navigation and decision paths
- accessible interactions across devices and assistive technologies
- friction-aware journeys and inclusive service design

### Understandable
Create experiences that are cognitively clear and emotionally coherent.

- plain language and consistent messaging
- predictable brand behavior and customer journeys
- transparent choices and reduced ambiguity

### Robust
Build systems that continue to work across evolving technologies, audiences, and contexts.

- scalable design systems
- resilient content frameworks
- compatibility with emerging tools and access needs
